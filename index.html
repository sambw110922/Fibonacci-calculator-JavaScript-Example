Fibonacci Calculator

About

In maths, the Fibonacci sequence is a term for a pattern of numbers where the next number in the sequence is the sum of the two previous. For example, in a sequence of 2, 3, n... , n will be 2 + 3, which is 5. The sequence repeats.

In this calculator, you enter the first two numbers of the sequence (minimum 0, maximum 100) and then the number of times to repeat (minimum 0, maximum 50). The results will then be displayed.
